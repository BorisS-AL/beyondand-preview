About

A Finance Transformation Partner.

Beyond& is a dedicated finance firm built to serve US mid-market companies with operational excellence, specialized expertise, and long-term partnership. Sponsor-grade finance & advisory, run as one accountable conversation.

The story.

Beyond& was established in 2026 as a joint venture between two organizations with complementary expertise: Arthur Lawrence, a 25-year-old US finance advisory firm, and The BPO, a world-class delivery and operations firm.

Rather than remain separate firms serving similar clients, the partners chose integration. They combined:

  • · Arthur Lawrence’s 25+ years of deep US market presence, client relationships, and finance transformation expertise.
  • · The BPO’s world-class delivery operations with proven ability to scale quality execution globally.
  • · Specialized expertise across accounting, FP&A, risk, transactions, tax, and advisory.

The result: Beyond& — one integrated firm with one culture, one operating model, and one commitment to finance transformation for US mid-market companies.

We operate as a unified organization with a Center of Excellence model. Our team is organized by practice area — not by legacy firm. Our clients work with Beyond&, not with “AL” or “The BPO.”

This integration is the whole point. We exist because separate firms serving mid-market companies create friction. Different cultures. Different standards. Handoffs between teams. Beyond& eliminates that friction by being truly integrated.

To optimize finance organizations. Not processes. Not headcount. Organizations.

We help CFOs, controllers, risk officers, and boards move from crisis management to strategic partnership. From tactical to transformational. From hope to confidence.
