About

What Beyond& is.

A finance-and-advisory firm built around one idea — the most useful word in our brand is the one in the middle. We sell the conjunction.

Beyond& exists because most firms in this market sell a function. Bookkeeping. AP. Internal audit. Tax compliance. Valuation. The CFO who buys those functions ends up with a vendor stack — different point of contact for each line, different reporting cadences, different quality standards, no one accountable for the whole. We do not believe that is the work CFOs want. It is the work they have been forced to accept.

So we sell the conjunction. AP and predictive collections. Close and disclose. Diligence and the 100-day plan. The work clients increasingly want delivered through one accountable relationship instead of fragmented across half a dozen vendors.

Beyond& brings the discipline of a Big-4 audit firm — the pyramid that filters, the documentation that defends, the partner who owns the page — to work that has historically been sold as outsourcing. The result is a different operating model for the back office, not a cheaper version of the same one.

How we work.

BRM-led. Every engagement has a named Business Relationship Manager — one person, on the engagement summary, accountable for every outcome. Not a portal. Not a ticket queue. A relationship.

Outcome-led, not headcount-led. SLAs and KPIs are contractual, not aspirational. We commit to what changes — close cycle days, DSO, audit-prep cycle time, cost reduction — not just to who shows up.

Big-4-grade, sponsor-priced. The work product is structured the way an audit committee expects to read it. The cost structure is the one a sponsor expects to see. The discipline lives in the work, not in the bill.

One accountable conversation. The more of your back office you can run through one accountable relationship, the less time you spend stitching vendors together. The conjunction is the product.

The structure behind the brand.

Beyond& was formed to deliver finance and advisory work through one accountable relationship, not fragmented across two firms' service lines. Two parent firms contribute their respective strengths to a single client-facing surface — one diagnostic, one named partner, one set of certifications underwriting the work, one bench operating against the calendar your audit committee actually reads.

Internal arrangements between the parents produce what clients see. Clients work with Beyond&. The trust stack below is what stands behind the conjunction.
