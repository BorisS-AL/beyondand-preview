Governance · POV 02

What Big-4-grade governance actually inherits from Big 4.

The phrase is meant to carry weight on the methodology side and on the partner-accountability side. It is not meant to carry weight on the org chart, the rate card, or the deck-deck-deck cadence Big 4 sometimes signals.

Three things are worth inheriting from Big 4. The audit-grade methodology — workpaper discipline, sampling defensibility, audit-trail rigour. The judgment that comes from running the methodology across hundreds of engagements. And the partner accountability that puts a single named person on the audit-committee report and the engagement summary.

What's not worth inheriting: the matrix-org consulting structure, the associate-pyramid economics, the sixty-page deck for a question that needs three pages, and the rate card that prices out the middle market entirely.

When Beyond& says a partner carries the engagement summary, the phrase is literal. The partner is the relationship anchor. The partner is on the audit-committee call. The partner is the person whose name appears under the deliverable. That structural choice is what makes Big-4-grade governance actually work for clients who don't have Big-4 economics.

The conjunction here is methodology & accountability. Without methodology, accountability is theatre. Without accountability, methodology is paperwork. Together they're the inheritance.

Everything else is rate-card baggage.
