IA & Continuous Monitoring.

Annual audit cycles are a sampling problem. Continuous monitoring is the answer most CFOs already know they need. Co-sourced or fully managed internal audit, fraud and forensic, external audit back-office support — with monitoring built on existing ERP and HRIS feeds.

You test some transactions, hope the ones you didn't test look like the ones you did, and call it a year. The auditor does the same thing, and both of you carry the gap between what you sampled and what actually happened.

The conjunction closes that gap. Rules sit on top of the ERP and HRIS feeds you already have, flagging exceptions in the week they happen instead of in the audit twelve months later. Annual IA work shifts from "find what we missed" to "confirm what we already know."

Co-sourced or fully managed. Named partner on the engagement summary. The auditor stops asking the same questions every quarter because the answers are in the workpaper trail by the time the question lands.

What lands here.

  • Co-sourced IA. Our bench augments your in-house team. Methodology + tooling we bring; sector knowledge you have.
  • Fully managed IA. End-to-end internal audit. Risk assessment, audit plan, fieldwork, reporting.
  • Continuous monitoring. Rules on ERP and HRIS. Exceptions flagged weekly. Audit becomes confirmation, not discovery.
  • Fraud & forensic. Targeted investigations. Litigation-ready forensic accounting.
  • External audit back-office. Workpaper prep, schedule-building, audit-firm liaison.
  • SOX scoping. Annual refresh, RCM build, quarterly key-control testing.
