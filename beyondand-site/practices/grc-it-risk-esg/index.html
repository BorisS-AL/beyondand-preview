06 — Practice

Controls & Conviction.

Governance you can show your investors. Controls your IT auditor can actually trace. ESG that survives a sponsor's diligence. GRC, ITGC, VAPT, BC/DR, data privacy — plus ESG strategy, reporting, controls and metrics.

Controls aren't paperwork — they're the receipts your auditor and your sponsor both want to see. The mistake most growth-stage CFOs make is treating GRC like a documentation exercise. The auditor isn't reading the binder for prose; they're looking for evidence the control fired.

The conjunction builds controls the way auditors trace them. ITGC documented at the configuration level. SOX narratives that name the system, the owner, and the evidence trail. VAPT scheduled before the next sponsor letter, not the next quarter. BC/DR tested, not asserted.

ESG sits in the same family. Strategy, reporting, controls, metrics — built so the next sponsor diligence isn't a scramble. The certifications in the trust stack earn their place.

What lands here.

  • GRC programs. Governance frameworks, risk taxonomies, control libraries with named ownership.
  • ITGC documentation. Configuration-level ITGC. Logical access, change management, computer ops — mapped to audited systems.
  • VAPT. Vulnerability assessment + penetration testing on the audit calendar.
  • BC/DR. Business continuity + disaster recovery. Tabletops that actually happen.
  • Data privacy. GDPR-aligned, CCPA-aware, sector-specific privacy programs.
  • ESG strategy & reporting. Materiality assessment, metrics dashboard, sponsor-grade narratives.
