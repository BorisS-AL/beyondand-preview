Diligence & 100-Day Plan.

Diligence asks what's wrong. The 100-day plan decides what to do about it. Same team. One narrative. Quality of earnings, purchase price allocation, 409a, fair value, ESOP — plus the integration plan that lands before the wire does.

Most firms hand off between diligence and integration, and the calendar slip happens in the handoff. The conjunction doesn't hand off. The diligence team and the 100-day team are the same team. Same partner. Same model. Same narrative.

57 named deliverables across the valuation and modelling bench. QoE, QoFP, FDD, SPA review on the deal timeline you actually have. Carve-out, add-on, and exit-prep delivery — built around the calendar your sponsor expects to see, not the calendar that suits the firm.

The integration plan lands before the wire does, because the people writing it have been reading the QoE for weeks. One narrative, end to end.

What lands here.

  • Quality of earnings (QoE). Sell-side or buy-side. Full normalization workbook. Defended in audit.
  • Quality of forecast (QoFP). Sponsor-case stress-test of the management plan.
  • Financial due diligence. Full FDD across a target. Tax-, IT-, HR-diligence coordination on multi-discipline mandates.
  • SPA / earnouts / escrow review. Working-capital target calibration. Earnout-mechanics input.
  • Valuation work. PPA, 409a, DCF, Monte Carlo, OPM, PWERM. ESOP and fair-value support.
  • 100-day plan + integration. Operating cadence for the first hundred days. Same team that ran the diligence.
