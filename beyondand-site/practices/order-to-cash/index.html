AP, AR & Predictive Collections.

We don't just process invoices. We tell you which ones won't get paid on time — before the dunning cycle starts. Full P2P and O2C operating model, US hours covered, AI classification and anomaly detection built in.

AR and AP look like back-office plumbing until they aren't. The day your DSO drifts past sixty is the day the operating-cash conversation with your sponsor changes tone — quietly at first, then less quietly.

The conjunction rebuilds order-to-cash and procure-to-pay as one operating model. AI sits on top of the invoices your AP clerk is already looking at, flagging the ones that will slip before the dunning cycle starts. Reconciliation queues shrink. Exceptions stop being a Monday-morning surprise.

Senior controllers, named BRM, US-hours coverage. The clerk goes on to harder work; the AI does the pattern-matching it's actually good at.

What lands here.

  • Full P2P operating model. Invoice intake, three-way match, exception routing, payment discipline.
  • Full O2C operating model. Order capture through cash application. Audit-defended customer-master discipline.
  • AI invoice classification. Anomaly detection, duplicate-payment elimination, at-risk flagging.
  • Predictive collections. Risk-scored AR aging; targeted outreach where it actually moves cash.
  • DSO compression. 75 to 43 days achievable; 90% reduction in unapplied cash.
  • Working-capital reporting. Daily AR/AP dashboard the CFO actually opens.

Working capital isn't a topic for the board. It's a balance you read every morning.
