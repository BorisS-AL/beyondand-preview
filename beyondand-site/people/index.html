People

Named partners. Named bench.

Every Beyond& engagement carries a named partner on the engagement summary — from the diagnostic call through delivery. The bench underneath is named too.

The named-partner discipline is structural. The partner who took the diagnostic call is the partner who runs the bench on the account, and the partner who shows up on the audit-committee report when audit time arrives. We don't run BD-to-delivery handoffs.

Full bios with credentials and alumni firms live on the Arthur Lawrence FAS surface and on theBPO's site, since both parent firms contribute partners to Beyond& engagements. The Beyond& brand is the conjunction; the partner bench is what makes the conjunction land.

What the bench looks like.

Partner-level relationship anchors. Named on every engagement summary. Big-4 lineage anchored in Talib's EY Americas Commodity Trading Leader tenure; Houston-rooted, US-facing.

Named BRMs. Single point of escalation per engagement. The BRM sits inside the operating cadence, not adjacent to it.

Senior controllers. Audit-firm lineage on the bench. Workpaper discipline that survives an audit-committee review.

Valuation & diligence bench. 57 named deliverables across PPA, 409a, DCF, Monte Carlo, OPM, PWERM, ESOP, and fair-value work.

For named individuals and full credentials, the AL FAS People page is the most current canonical source — al-fas-preview.netlify.app/people/.
